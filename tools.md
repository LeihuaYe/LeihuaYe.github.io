## Experimentation and causal inference

<div class="card">
  <h3><a href="https://github.com/LeihuaYe/claude-experimentation">claude-experimentation</a></h3>
  <p>A trustworthy A/B test readout as a Claude Code skill: sample-ratio check, CUPED variance reduction, effect and confidence interval, Benjamini-Hochberg correction, and a ship/no-ship verdict. Statistics are validated against simulations with known ground truth.</p>

# About

I'm Leihua Ye, a senior data scientist in Silicon Valley. I have 10+ years of research and industry experience in experimentation, causal inference, and statistics.

## What I work on

- **Experimentation at scale:** variance reduction (CUPED), sequential testing, and analysis methods for experiments that span online and offline channels.
- **Statistical consulting:** helping product and business teams pick the right method for their experiment, and building a culture of trustworthy testing.
- **Building with AI:** using Claude and agent workflows to automate data science and research.

## Background

- **PhD, Political Science**, UC Santa Barbara, 2022. My dissertation combined matching with difference-in-differences to estimate the policy effects of village elections in rural China.
- **MA, Statistics and Applied Probability**, UC Santa Barbara, 2018.
- I've earned degrees across the humanities, social sciences, and STEM in three countries: China, the UK, and the US.
